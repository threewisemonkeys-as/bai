AW9WD — Cracking eggshell / liquid-fall dynamics

GRID
- 16 x 16 grid. Positions are (x, y); y grows downward, so row 15 is the bottom.

OBJECTS
- Eggshell: "tan" colored cell, has a boolean field `broken` (starts false).
  37 eggshells are placed at init, arranged as an egg/oval shape occupying
  roughly rows 9-15 (e.g. (7,15),(8,15),(9,15) up through (7,9),(8,9),(9,9)).
  Together they form a solid tan egg.
- Feather: a cell with a boolean `hidden` (all start true) and a color (mix of
  "orange" and "yellow"). Exactly 18 feathers are placed at init underneath the
  egg, forming a chick shape (rows 10-15). Every feather sits at a position that
  also has an eggshell, so at init each feather is occluded by the tan eggshell
  rendered on top of it (eggshells are declared after feathers, so they draw
  over them). Feathers ARE visible objects rendered in their own color — they
  show up as orange/yellow cells as soon as the eggshell covering them is gone.
  They never move. The `hidden` field is never toggled by any rule and has no
  effect on rendering: rendering is purely occlusion (top object wins), so a
  feather appears the moment the eggshell at its cell is removed, even though
  `hidden` is still true.

PLAYER ACTIONS
- click: the ONLY meaningful action. Clicking on an eggshell cell sets that
  eggshell's `broken` field to true. Click LOCATION matters — only the
  eggshell at the clicked cell is affected (clicked eggshell -> broken=true).
  Clicking empty space or a feather does nothing.
- left, right, up, down: NO-OPS. There are no movement handlers for any
  arrow/direction action; nothing in the program responds to them.
- noop: nothing happens.

PASSIVE / CLOCK DYNAMICS (every step, via nextEggshells -> nextLiquid)
- feathers are static: feathers(next) = feathers(prev). They never change.
- eggshells each step are split into:
    * unbroken eggshells (broken=false): stay exactly in place.
    * broken eggshells (broken=true): they "fall" like a liquid via nextLiquid:
        - if it is not on the bottom row and the cell directly below is free,
          the eggshell moves down one cell.
        - otherwise it takes one step toward the nearest reachable "hole" — a
          free cell in the row below whose cell above is also free (liquid
          spreading sideways/diagonally); if no hole is reachable, it stays.
  Occupied cells (unbroken eggshells, other broken eggshells) block the fall, so
  a broken eggshell drains downward/sideways and piles up at the bottom of the
  grid, settling on the floor (row 15) or on top of other already-settled
  eggshells. NOTE: broken eggshells do NOT come to rest on feather cells — they
  are despawned the step they overlap a feather (see SPAWN/DESPAWN).

SPAWN / DESPAWN
- No spawning. Feathers are fixed and never removed. Eggshells: unbroken ones
  stay put; broken ones relocate (fall) AND can be despawned.
- DESPAWN of eggshells (this is the key mechanic): in nextEggshells the broken
  eggshells are first filtered by `(! (intersects es feathers))`, i.e. any
  broken eggshell occupying a feather cell is dropped before nextLiquid runs.
  This happens in two ways:
    * Clicking an eggshell that sits directly on a feather (all 18 feather
      cells have an eggshell on top at init): it breaks, then is removed next
      step, immediately uncovering that feather.
    * A broken eggshell that drains down/sideways onto a feather cell: it is
      removed the step it lands there.
  Either way the eggshell vanishes and the feather underneath becomes visible.

COLOR CHANGES
- None. Eggshells stay "tan"; feathers keep their fixed colors. Breaking an
  eggshell changes its `broken` flag and its behavior (it starts falling) but
  not its rendered color.

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS
- Falling broken eggshells collide with grid bounds, the floor, and other
  eggshells (occupied cells block the liquid free-cell checks). Feathers are NOT
  a resting surface: an eggshell reaching a feather cell is despawned, not
  stacked. Eggshell-vs-feather (despawn/uncover) and eggshell-vs-eggshell
  (liquid blocking) are the only object-object interactions.

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD
- None. There is no goal, win condition, termination rule, or reward defined.
  The game is purely a sandbox: click to break the tan egg shell and watch the
  broken pieces drain away, gradually uncovering the chick-shaped region below.
