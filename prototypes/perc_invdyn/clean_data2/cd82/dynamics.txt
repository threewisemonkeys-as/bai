cd82 (game_id cd82-fb555c5d) — game mechanics

OVERVIEW
- A "paint the canvas to match the target" puzzle. A 10x10 TARGET picture is shown
  top-left; a blank 10x10 CANVAS sits in the center. You drive a "basket / paint
  applicator" around 8 positions on a ring surrounding the canvas, pick a color
  from a palette at the top, and STAMP that color onto the canvas from the side
  the basket currently occupies. When the canvas matches the target (ignoring the
  two main diagonals) the level is solved and you advance to the next level.
- 6 levels (info win_levels=6). Solving a level yields reward +0.1 and loads the
  next level. Running out of the action budget (100 actions) loses the game.

GRID & LAYERS
- One 64x64 grid layer per observation (the engine renders only the final settled
  frame after any multi-tick stamp/preview animation; <grid_0> only).
- Coordinates are (x=col, y=row); x grows rightward, y grows downward.
- camera scale = 1, offset = 0, so DISPLAY coords == GRID coords: a click
  `click_X_Y` (-> ACTION6 x=X y=Y) targets grid column X, row Y directly.

PALETTE (confirmed indices)
- 0  = white   (canvas default fill; a selectable swatch color)
- 2  = light gray (basket / applicator outline)
- 3  = gray    (decorative background frame `gkyfnkvrty`, the top bar)
- 4  = dark gray (letterbox padding, swatch borders, the step-budget bar at row 63)
- 5  = black   (camera background)
- 12 = orange  (target/swatch color, appears from level 2)
- 15 = purple  (default selected color; a swatch color)
- 8, 9, 11, 14 = additional swatch/target colors used in levels 3-6 (not visually
  named here, but they are palette indices remapped into the swatches at level 3+).

ON-SCREEN OBJECTS (level 1)
- TARGET picture: 10x10 sprite `eoqnvkspoa-pqwme<level>-1` at grid (col 3, row 3),
  i.e. occupying cols 3..12, rows 3..12, behind a gray frame. Level 1 target =
  top 5 rows white (0), bottom 5 rows purple (15). This is the goal image.
- CANVAS: 10x10 sprite `xytrjjbyib` at (col 27, row 34), cols 27..36, rows 34..43.
  Starts entirely white (0). This is what you paint and what is compared to the
  target.
- BASKET / applicator: sprite "ActiveBasket" (a `oaoosfneq-*` shape, light-gray
  outline color 2, interior filled with the CURRENTLY SELECTED color). It sits at
  one of 8 ring positions around the canvas and shows which color you will stamp.
- PALETTE swatches: `pqkenviek` sprites, 5x5 squares (dark-gray border color 4,
  3x3 colored center) in a row near the top. Level 1 has 2 swatches: white (center
  color 0) at (col 35, row 2) and purple (center 15) at (col 41, row 2). The center
  cell of a swatch is the color it selects.
- SELECTION MARKER: `ydiwkzjkgl`, a 1x5 underline placed just below the currently
  selected swatch (starts under the purple swatch -> selected color = 15 at reset).

THE 8-POSITION SELECTOR RING
- The basket lives at one of 8 ring positions, indexed 0..7, laid out as the 8
  outer cells of a 3x3 grid (the center (1,1) is unused/blocked):
        idx7 (0,0)   idx0 (0,1)   idx1 (0,2)
        idx6 (1,0)    center      idx2 (1,2)
        idx5 (2,0)   idx4 (2,1)   idx3 (2,2)
  where the pair is (grid-row, grid-col) within the 3x3 selector, NOT screen cells.
  Reset starts at idx0 (top-middle, basket above the canvas).
- Screen placement of each idx (basket center, col,row) and the canvas region it
  stamps (see STAMP below):
    idx0 top         (25,24)  -> canvas TOP half     (rows 0..4)
    idx1 top-right   (33,21)  -> upper-right triangle (row i: cols i..9)
    idx2 right       (38,32)  -> canvas RIGHT half    (cols 5..9)
    idx3 bottom-right(33,40)  -> lower-right triangle (row i: cols 9-i..9)
    idx4 bottom      (25,45)  -> canvas BOTTOM half   (rows 5..9)
    idx5 bottom-left (14,40)  -> lower-left triangle  (row i: cols 0..i)
    idx6 left        (17,32)  -> canvas LEFT half     (cols 0..4)
    idx7 top-left    (14,21)  -> upper-left triangle  (row i: cols 0..9-i)

PLAYER ACTIONS (available_actions is constant [ACTION1..ACTION6] in every state)
- ACTION1 = move selector UP    (selector grid-row - 1)
- ACTION2 = move selector DOWN  (selector grid-row + 1)
- ACTION3 = move selector LEFT  (selector grid-col - 1)
- ACTION4 = move selector RIGHT (selector grid-col + 1)
  Movement is within the 3x3 selector and clamped to [0,2]. A move is REJECTED
  (basket stays put, frame unchanged) if it would land on the blocked center (1,1)
  or off the edge. Consequence: from idx0 (top-middle) you cannot go straight up or
  down — you must step sideways first (e.g. ACTION4 -> idx1, then ACTION2 -> idx2).
  Each accepted move teleports the basket to the new ring position and refills its
  interior with the selected color.
- ACTION5 = STAMP. Plays a dip animation: the basket lunges into the canvas from
  its current side and at the apex paints the SELECTED COLOR over the canvas region
  for the current ring position (the TOP/BOTTOM/LEFT/RIGHT half for cardinal
  positions, or a corner TRIANGLE for diagonal positions; see ring table), then
  retracts. Only the final painted canvas is rendered. This is the core paint tool.
- ACTION6 = CLICK at (x=col, y=row). Two distinct targets:
    (a) Click a palette swatch (`pqkenviek`): sets the selected color to that
        swatch's center color, moves the underline marker beneath it, and refills
        the basket interior with the new color. Click the swatch CENTER, e.g. the
        level-1 white swatch center is (col 37, row 4) -> `click_37_4`, the purple
        swatch center is (col 43, row 4) -> `click_43_4`.
    (b) Click a hint ARROW (`ctwspzkygu`, present only in levels 3-6 and only when
        the basket is at a cardinal position idx 0/2/4/6): plays a nudge animation
        and paints a SMALL central edge STRIP (a 3x4 / 4x3 block on that side:
        top=rows0-2/cols3-6, bottom=rows7-9/cols3-6, left=cols0-2/rows3-6,
        right=cols7-9/rows3-6) with the selected color — a finer paint than the
        full-half ACTION5 stamp. Not exercised in this dataset's level-1 trajectories.
  Clicking empty space or a non-interactive cell does nothing.

CLICK / COORDINATE CONVENTION
- `click_X_Y` on the CLI -> ACTION6 x=X y=Y, with X = column, Y = row (display ==
  grid here). To select a swatch, click its center cell. To click an arrow, click
  on the arrow sprite's cells.

WIN / LEVEL / SCORE
- After every ACTION5 stamp (and after an arrow-strip paint) the canvas is compared
  to the target. The comparison IGNORES the two main diagonals of the 10x10 (cells
  (i,i) and (i,9-i) are "don't care"); all other cells must match exactly. On a
  match the level is solved: reward +0.1 and the next level loads (basket reset to
  idx0, selected color reset to 15/purple, fresh white canvas, new target).
- Level 1 solution: the canvas already starts white (matching the target's white
  top half), and the default selected color is purple, so simply move the basket to
  the BOTTOM (idx4) and ACTION5 to stamp the bottom half purple -> match -> advance.
- Levels 2-6 use multi-region targets (diagonal splits, multiple colors, the orange
  12 and other indices) requiring several color-select + stamp combinations and, in
  levels 3-6, the arrow strip tool; they are not solved in this dataset.
- LOSE: a fixed budget of 100 actions (the bar along grid row 63 shrinks each
  action: filled portion = dark gray 4, remaining = black 5); exhausting it ends
  the episode as a loss. No other termination.

NOTES / CAVEATS
- available_actions never changes (always all six), even mid-animation; the engine
  runs each stamp/preview animation to completion inside a single env.step, so each
  emitted action maps to exactly one rendered observation.
- A rejected move (into the center, off-edge, or a redundant re-select click) is a
  legal no-op: the frame is unchanged but the action is consumed from the budget.
