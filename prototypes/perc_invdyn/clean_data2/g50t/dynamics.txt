g50t (game_id g50t-5849a774) — game mechanics

OVERVIEW
- A grid Sokoban / "time-recording" puzzle. You steer a single avatar through a
  walled maze to an exit, but the exit is gated by a door that is held open only
  while a pressure-button is occupied. Since the avatar can only be in one place,
  the signature mechanic is RECORD-AND-REWIND (ACTION5): you record a short path,
  rewind it, and a replaying "ghost" of yourself walks that path while you take
  fresh control from the start — the ghost holds the button so you can pass.
- The game is a sequence of 7 self-contained levels of increasing complexity.
  Completing a level (avatar reaches the exit) advances to the next and pays a
  small reward. This clean-data set covers LEVEL 1, the canonical introduction.

GRID & LAYERS
- 64x64 cell grid, single rendered layer (layers=1). 16-color palette, index 0
  = black background, index 2 = padding/letterbox border.
- The world uses a COARSE movement lattice with cell size 6: the avatar's
  top-left x and y are always multiples-of-6 offsets from its start. In Level 1
  the avatar starts at (x=13, y=7) and every move changes x or y by exactly 6.
- Coordinates here are (X=col, Y=row); X grows rightward, Y grows downward.
  (No clicks are used by this game, but the convention would be ACTION6 x=col,
  y=row.)

PALETTE / OBJECTS (raw grid color -> appearance -> meaning)
- 9  = blue. The AVATAR (player) is a 7x7 blue block with a single color-5
  center pixel, start (13,7). The EXIT/GOAL is also blue: a 9x9 hollow blue
  square with a center dot, at (42,48) in Level 1.
- 8  = red. The BUTTON (a 3x3 red pad, at (37,7) in L1), the DOOR (a red plug/
  bar that physically blocks a corridor, at (13,37) in L1), and the red WIRE
  line that visually connects a button to the door it controls. Door and button
  linked by a wire share state.
- 2  = gray. A GHOST/CLONE: a former avatar turned into a gray 7x7 block (color-5
  center) that replays a recorded path. Created by ACTION5.
- 1  = blue (top-left HUD). The CLONE-BUDGET counters: small filled squares at
  the top-left showing how many recordings you may still spawn this level. Level
  1 has 2 counters; Levels 2-7 have 3. The currently-armed counter is lit.
- 5  = the WALKABLE FLOOR mask (light). The avatar's CENTER cell must lie on a
  floor pixel; black (0) cells are walls/void and block movement. Each level has
  its own floor layout (a maze of corridors and rooms).
- A thin colored bar along the very bottom row is a TIME bar that scrolls left
  as you act (see WIN/LOSE).

PLAYER ACTIONS (available set is constant: ACTION1,2,3,4,5 — movement only)
- ACTION1 = move UP    (y -= 6)
- ACTION2 = move DOWN  (y += 6)
- ACTION3 = move LEFT  (x -= 6)
- ACTION4 = move RIGHT (x += 6)
  A move only succeeds if the destination keeps the avatar's center on the floor
  mask AND not inside a currently-closed (visible) door. Otherwise the action is
  a no-op (the avatar does not move). No clock advances purely from movement
  besides the time bar.
- ACTION5 = RECORD / REWIND (the clone action). It takes the list of moves you
  have made since the start of the level, rewinds the avatar back along that
  path to its start position, consumes one clone-budget counter, and spawns a
  GHOST that will replay exactly those recorded moves. You then resume control of
  a fresh avatar from the start. The ghost's replay is move-synchronized: ghost
  move k fires on your k-th subsequent move; once the ghost exhausts its recorded
  moves it STAYS put (e.g. parked on a button), holding its final position for
  the rest of the level. If you ever use up all clone-budget counters, the ghosts
  are cleared and the budget resets.
- There is no click action and no ACTION6/7 in this game.

BUTTON / DOOR (the gate mechanic)
- Stepping the avatar's (or a ghost's) center onto a BUTTON pad sets the button
  "pressed", which slides its linked DOOR aside (out of the corridor it blocks),
  opening the passage. In Level 1 the button at (37,7) is wired to the door at
  (13,37): pressing it slides the door ~6 cells right (its red plug moves from
  cols 14-18 to cols 20-24), clearing the left vertical corridor (cols 13-19).
- The door re-closes shortly after the button is vacated. Because the button and
  the exit are far apart, a single avatar cannot hold the button and reach the
  exit; you must leave a GHOST on the button (via ACTION5) and walk the real
  avatar through while it is held open.

LEVEL 1 LAYOUT (concrete)
- Avatar start (13,7), top-left. Top corridor runs along row 7 from col 13 to
  col 43. The button sits at the right end of the top corridor (37,7).
- The LEFT vertical corridor (cols 13-19) runs from the top corridor down to the
  bottom corridor (row ~49), but is blocked partway by the DOOR at (13,37).
- A middle ROOM (roughly rows 19-25, cols 13-31, with prongs up cols 13-19 and
  25-31, and a row-13 corridor cols 13-37) sits above the door.
- The BOTTOM corridor runs along row 49 from col 13 east to the EXIT at the
  bottom-right. The EXIT/GOAL is at (42,48); the win cell is (43,49).

WIN / LOSE / SCORE / REWARD
- WIN a level when the avatar's top-left equals goal+(1,1) — in Level 1 exactly
  (43,49). This ends the level, pays reward = 0.1, and loads the next level
  (term stays False until the final, 7th level is cleared, which ends the game).
- LOSE if (a) the bottom TIME bar scrolls fully off-screen — it moves left by 1
  every 2 actions, so you have on the order of ~130 actions per level — or
  (b) the avatar is destroyed/dissolved (later levels add patrolling hazards and
  closing doors that can crush the avatar). Neither lose condition is exercised
  in this Level-1 clean data.
- Reward is 0 on every ordinary step and 0.1 on the step that completes a level.

NOTES / CAVEATS
- The clone setup in Level 1 is essentially forced: the only way to occupy the
  button is the 4 rights along the top corridor (13->37) followed by ACTION5, so
  both clean trajectories share that opening; they diverge entirely in the
  solving route afterward.
- An attempted move into a wall or a closed door is silently dropped (no-op, no
  state change); the trajectories here avoid such no-ops.
- The avatar cannot descend past row 49 in the left corridor (its center would
  leave the floor), so an extra DOWN at the bottom is a no-op — avoided here.
