S2KT7 - Ant foraging simulation (16x16 grid)

Coordinates below are (row, col), matching what the perception/observation reports.

GRID
- 16x16 cell grid. Empty cells are black.

OBJECTS
- Ant: gray cell. Exactly two ants exist at start, at (row, col) = (5,5) and (14,1).
  (These come from the program's Position(x,y) values (5,5) and (1,14); written as
  (row, col) the second ant is at (14,1).)
- Food: red cell. None exist at start; created only by clicking (see below).

PLAYER ACTIONS
- click ROW COL: spawns food. IMPORTANT: the food always appears at the SAME FIXED cell
  (0,0) (top-left corner), regardless of which ROW/COL you click. The click LOCATION is
  irrelevant - only the fact that a click occurred matters.
  Why: the program adds food via `randomPositions`, which is seed-deterministic. Under
  seed 0 the spawned position(s) resolve to (0,0), so food deterministically materializes
  at (0,0) on every click. (The program nominally requests 2 random positions, but under
  seed 0 they coincide at (0,0) and render as a single red cell.)
  - Clicking while a food already exists at (0,0) produces no visible change (still one red
    cell at (0,0)).
  - If an ant is already sitting on (0,0) when you click, the new food is consumed almost
    immediately (the ant on that cell eats it within one tick), so it may never appear as a
    free red cell.
- left, right, up, down: NO handler. These are no-ops (no rule responds to directional input).
- noop: no-op (no handler).
Note: the only player action with any effect on world state is click; movement keys do nothing.

PASSIVE / CLOCK DYNAMICS (run every step via `on true`)
- Ant movement: every tick, each ant moves one cell toward its nearest food (the closest
  food to that ant), using the unit vector from the ant to that food. Both ants share the
  same global food set; each heads toward whichever food is nearest to it.
- Food removal (eating): a food is removed once an ant occupies its cell. In the rendering,
  the food cell shows red on the tick an ant arrives onto it, and the food disappears on the
  following tick (the filter removes food that intersected the ant's previous position).
- If no food exists, ants have no target and stay put (idle). After a food is eaten, both
  ants remain stationary at wherever they ended up until a new food is spawned by a click.

SPAWNING / DESPAWNING
- Spawn: Food spawns only on click, always at the fixed cell (0,0). It does not spawn over
  time or at random visible locations.
- Despawn: Food despawns when an ant reaches/overlaps its cell (eaten).
- Ants are never created or destroyed after init; always exactly two ants.

COLOR CHANGES
- None. Ants stay gray, food stays red throughout.

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS
- Ant-on-Food overlap removes the food (the only interaction in the game).
- No ant-ant collision handling; ants can share a cell freely.

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD
- None. There is no goal, win condition, termination rule, or reward in the program.
  It is an open-ended simulation: click anywhere to drop a food at (0,0), and the two ants
  converge on (0,0) and consume it.
