world knowledge
- Coordinates are given as [row, column] with rows increasing downward and columns increasing rightward.
- The agent is represented by the darkgreen cells.
- Each action moves the agent one step in the corresponding direction:
  - left: column decreases by 1 (row unchanged)
  - right: column increases by 1 (row unchanged)
  - up: row decreases by 1 (column unchanged)
  - down: row increases by 1 (column unchanged)
