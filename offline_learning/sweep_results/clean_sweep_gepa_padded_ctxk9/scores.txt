Overall
-------
Mean 2.29 across all 21 games. Distribution is dominated by 2s (12/21);
one collapse (aw9wd=1, perception degenerated to grid_not_found) and one strong result
(e3v6m=4). On the 15 games also scored in the prior within-trajectory ctxk3 run, this run is
essentially flat (≈2.27 vs 2.20): 4 up (83wkq 1->2, ice 1->2, qfsvc 2->3, s2kt7 2->3), 3 down
(aw9wd 2->1, bt3gb 3->2, f5w3n 3->2), 8 unchanged.

Key finding: the noop-padding + context_k=9 did NOT materially improve belief quality, and
in particular did not fix the dominant failure mode it was meant to target -- PASSIVE / CLOCK
dynamics are still consistently missed or mis-modeled: per-tick contagion (dq8gc), cook+gas
burn (27vwc), random-walk diffusion (83wkq), crate sinking (nrdf6), ant foraging (s2kt7),
persistent gravity (vqjh6), leaf growth (7xf97). The padding supplies clean passive baselines
in the windows, but the learned beliefs still fail to articulate the underlying passive laws;
several runs instead invent deterministic surrogates (83wkq's "shift left", nrdf6's step%4,
ada85's timer toggle). The recurring structural failures remain: (1) perception collapse to a
constant/blank output zeroes a game (aw9wd and va6fq here), and (2) interactive/economy mechanics
(ammo in n2ntd/qfsvc, magnetism in 7www9) and click-location semantics are under-learned.
