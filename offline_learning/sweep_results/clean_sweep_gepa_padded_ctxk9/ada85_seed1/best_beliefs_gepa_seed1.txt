The environment is a 11x11 grid with black background. Two immutable blue cells at (0,0) and red at (10,0). Two spawn locations: Spawn(0,0) and Spawn(10,0). Each spawn has state "ready" or "active". Autonomous gray objects move stepwise in a cardinal direction. Patterns A and B alternate column 10 colors on a timer.

Pattern A colors (col10): row3=white, row4=yellow, row5=yellow, row6=yellow, row7=white.
Pattern B colors (col10): row3=yellow, row4=white, row5=gold, row6=white, row7=yellow.

Pattern auto-change at a scheduled step (e.g. step 20, step 40, step 60) toggles pattern and updates column 10 colors accordingly.

Autonomous objects move one cell per step in their current direction. When an autonomous object reaches the row0 or row10 boundary while moving north/south, or col0 or col10 boundary while moving east/west, it turns at the boundary:
- Moving east and reaching col10 → turn north (dir=north), next cell is (row-1, col=10).
- Moving west and reaching col0 → turn south (dir=south), next cell is (row+1, col=0).
- Moving north and reaching row0 → turn east (dir=east), next cell is (row=0, col+1).
- Moving south and reaching row10 → turn west (dir=west), next cell is (row=10, col-1).

Autonomous objects move through, not over, column 10 colored cells; they occupy those cells and coexist with the pattern color.

When an autonomous object moves off the grid or disappears (e.g. after entering column 10 and then moving north past row0? actually row0 is top boundary, row10 bottom; object disappears when it moves to the cell just after the last valid cell along its path based on boundary turning rules — but from examples, objects that enter col10 then move north from row0? not seen; objects continue until they reach bottom-left spawn area and vanish), the spawn that produced it returns to "ready" state, and no autonomous object remains.

Clicking a spawn at (0,0) or (10,0) while it is "ready":
- Activates that spawn (changes to "active") and spawns a new gray autonomous object at the spawn cell.
- The autonomous object's initial direction and next cell:
  - Spawn(0,0): object at (0,0) dir=east next=(0,1) [but note (0,0) is blue immutable; object appears adjacent at (0,1) dir=east? from examples: after clicking (0,0) when ready, next state shows spawn active and gray at (0,1) dir=east next=(0,2)].
  - Spawn(10,0): object at (10,0) dir=east next=(10,1) [similarly, object at (10,1) dir=east].
- Clicking a spawn that is "active" has no effect (remains active with the object already moving).

Clicking on column 10 cells (pattern area) toggles pattern:
- Clicking the gold cell (row5,col10) in pattern B toggles pattern to A and updates column 10 colors to pattern A's set.
- Clicking any of pattern A's yellow or white cells at col10 toggles pattern to B and updates column 10 colors to pattern B's set.
- Clicking elsewhere on col10 that is not the pattern's key cell triggers pattern toggle as well (based on pattern, not on exact cell).
- After manual toggle via click, pattern auto-change step counter resets? (from examples: after manual toggle pattern at step 45→46, pattern auto-change at step 60 for the new pattern).

noop action: all autonomous objects move one step in their direction (with boundary turning), pattern auto-change timer advances (if timer reaches scheduled step, pattern toggles automatically), spawn states remain unchanged (active spawns stay active, ready spawns stay ready). No other state changes.

up/down/left/right actions have no effect on state (agent movement not relevant in this environment).
