F5W3N — Game Dynamics (Space-Invaders shooter; regenerated under NON-ZERO seeds)

Grid: 16x16, black background. Coords are (x, y) with y increasing DOWNWARD; the
render is grid[y][x], so "row" = y, "col" = x. Hero starts at (x=8, y=15), bottom-center.

WHY THIS DATASET EXISTS (the seed-0 bug):
  The sibling dataset clean_data3/f5w3n was generated at seed=0, which is PATHOLOGICAL
  for this game's enemy-fire randomness. Enemies auto-fire every 15 ticks (rule
  `time % 15 == 3`) at a target chosen by `uniformChoice` over the COMBINED enemy pool
  (enemies1 row y=1 UNION enemies2 row y=3 = 10 enemies). Under seed 0 `uniformChoice`
  collapses to always returning the SAME index-0 enemy, so every fire spawns at the
  enemies1 col-1 enemy; its only motion is the ±1 formation march, so the targets
  degenerate to a fixed 2-value alternation confined to a SINGLE row:
      seed 0 spawns (x,y) = (2,1),(1,1),(2,1),(1,1),... forever
  The y=3 row is NEVER hit and only 2/10 pool candidates ever fire.
  Under NON-ZERO seeds the draw is genuine: targets span BOTH enemy rows with real
  variety. This dataset (seeds train 101-108, test 301-302, test50 501-508) exercises
  the enemy-fire RNG properly. VERIFIED in the generated data:
      spawn-row histogram y=1: 16 fires, y=3: 10 fires  (both rows fired)
      14 distinct spawn cells: (1,1)(1,3)(2,1)(4,1)(4,3)(5,1)(7,1)(7,3)(8,1)(8,3)
                               (10,3)(11,1)(13,1)(14,1)
  vs seed-0's 2 cells {(1,1),(2,1)}, all y=1.
  See memory seed0-degenerate-data-gen and F5W3N.sexp.

OBJECTS (initial state, time=0):
- Hero: one cell, gray when alive / black (invisible) when dead. Boolean `alive` field.
  Starts at (x=8, y=15).
- Enemy enemies1: blue cells on row y=1 at every x where x%3==1 -> x=1,4,7,10,13.
- Enemy enemies2: blue cells on row y=3 at every x where x%3==2 -> x=2,5,8,11,14.
- Bullet: red cells. Hero's shots. NONE at start.
- EnemyBullet: orange cells. Enemy shots. NONE at start.

PLAYER ACTIONS (whitelist: left, right, up, noop -- NO click, NO down):
- left:  hero moves one cell left (x-1), blocked at the grid edge (moveLeftNoCollision).
- right: hero moves one cell right (x+1), blocked at the grid edge.
- up:    FIRES a red bullet from the hero's current cell, but ONLY if the hero is alive.
         On the fire step the red bullet is drawn on the hero's cell, briefly occluding
         the gray hero (renders as the bullet's color for that one frame; the gray hero
         reappears the next frame once the bullet climbs away). This is NOT death.
- down:  NO handler -- pure no-op (not in the whitelist).
- click: NO handler -- pure no-op; click location irrelevant (not in the whitelist).
- noop:  does nothing; the clock/passive dynamics still advance.

PASSIVE / CLOCK DYNAMICS (every tick):
- `time` increments by 1 each step (starts at 0). Actions do NOT consume RNG.
- Every step: all red (hero) bullets move UP (y-1); all orange (enemy) bullets move DOWN (y+1).
- Enemy formation march (time-keyed, hits 2 of every 10 steps, static the other 8):
    time%10==5: enemies1 move LEFT, enemies2 move RIGHT.
    time%10==0: enemies1 move RIGHT, enemies2 move LEFT.
  Each row just oscillates ±1 around its start columns (opposite directions per row);
  the formation never drifts. Uses plain moveLeft/moveRight (no edge collision).
- Enemy firing (time%15==3): if any enemy remains, ONE new orange bullet spawns at the
  current cell of a UNIFORMLY RANDOM remaining enemy (drawn from enemies1 U enemies2). If
  no enemies remain, nothing spawns. Under a proper (non-zero) seed the target is genuinely
  random over BOTH rows; under seed 0 it collapses (see top). In 20-step episodes the fires
  land at observed steps 4 and 19 (2 fire events per episode).

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS:
- Hero bullet ∩ enemy (either row): both removed (enemy destroyed by being shot). 1-step
  prev-delayed: they co-locate on frame N, removal fires on N->N+1.
- Hero ∩ enemy bullet: hero `alive`->false (gray -> black/invisible; can no longer fire).
  The orange bullet is NOT consumed -- it keeps descending.
- Hero bullet ∩ enemy bullet: both removed (red and orange shots cancel).

SPAWN / DESPAWN:
- Red bullets spawn on `up` (one per fire, at the hero cell, only while alive); despawn
  when they hit an enemy, an enemy bullet, or fly off the top edge.
- Orange bullets spawn on the timed enemy-fire rule (one per fire, at a remaining enemy's
  current cell); despawn ONLY when they hit a red bullet (NOT when they kill the hero) or
  when they fly off the bottom edge.
- Enemies only despawn when shot; never respawned.

COLOR CHANGES:
- Hero is gray while alive, black when killed (indistinguishable from empty background).

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD:
- No explicit win/loss/termination/reward. Reward is 0.0 every step. Hero death only
  disables firing; the sim keeps running (a dead hero still moves left/right, aliasing
  with noop since it is invisible). In this dataset only 3/18 episodes end in a real death,
  and always on the LAST transition (obs 18->19) when fire#1's bullet reaches the bottom row
  -- the hero is alive and visible for the rest of every episode.

IDENTIFIABILITY NOTE:
- `up` is distinguishable from noop (spawns a visible red bullet, even though the hero cell
  is briefly occluded on the fire frame). left/right are distinguishable while the hero is
  alive (gray cell shifts ±1 col). Enemy-fire and march are passive/time-keyed and best
  learned under FD; enemy-fire+ now has genuine both-row/varied-column targets (unlike the
  seed-0 data, where the fixed single-cell alternation let a step-counter fake it).
