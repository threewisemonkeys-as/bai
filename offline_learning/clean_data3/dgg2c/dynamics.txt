DGG2C — Game Dynamics

GRID
- 17 x 17 cell grid (GRID_SIZE = 17).

OBJECTS
- Cloud: a static band of gray cells. The SEXP builds it from the rectangle
  (Position 0 0)..(Position 17 2), which renders as the top TWO rows only —
  rows y=0 and y=1, spanning all 17 columns (x=0..16). Row y=2 is NOT gray
  (it is the empty/black row where water spawns). The cloud is created once at
  init and never moves or changes ("gray").
- Water: lightblue cells. The water list starts empty at init. Water cells are
  spawned periodically (see SPAWNING) and fall under gravity (see CLOCK).

STATE VARIABLES
- wind: integer, starts at 0. Clamped to the range [-1, 1].
- time: integer, starts at 0, increments by 1 every step (on true).

PLAYER ACTIONS
- left:  decreases wind by 1, but not below -1 (wind = max(wind-1, -1)).
- right: increases wind by 1, but not above 1 (wind = min(wind+1, 1)).
- up:    no handler — no-op.
- down:  no handler — no-op.
- click: no handler — no-op. Click LOCATION is irrelevant (no click rule).
- noop:  no effect.

CLOCK / PASSIVE DYNAMICS (per step)
- Gravity + wind drift on every step, driven by current wind value:
  - wind == 0:  every water cell moves down one row.
  - wind == 1:  every water cell moves down and right one cell (diagonal).
  - wind == -1: every water cell moves down and left one cell (diagonal).
- Water that moves outside the grid bounds is removed (filtered by
  isWithinBounds each step), so off-grid water despawns.

SPAWNING
- Whenever (time % 5) == 2 (i.e. periodically every 5 steps), four new water
  cells are added at row y=2 at columns x = 2, 6, 10, 14.
- This is the only spawn source; there is no despawn other than the
  out-of-bounds filter above.

COLOR CHANGES
- None. Cloud is always gray; water is always lightblue. No thresholds or
  color-change rules exist.

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS
- None. Water cells do not collide with the cloud, each other, or boundaries;
  they simply fall and are removed when off-grid. The cloud is purely visual.

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD
- None defined. There is no goal object, no win condition, no termination rule,
  and no reward in the SEXP. The simulation runs indefinitely.

SUMMARY
- Water "rain" spawns from a fixed row every 5 steps and falls; the player's
  only influence is steering the fall direction left/right/straight via wind.
