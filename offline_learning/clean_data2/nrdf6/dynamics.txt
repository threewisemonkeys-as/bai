NRDF6 — Game Dynamics

GRID: 7x7. Object cell positions in the SEXP are offsets relative to an
object's origin. The crate's origin starts near the top-center (Position 3,1).
The Rock object is a single-cell template; NO rocks exist at the start (the
rocks list is initialized empty) — rocks only ever appear via clicks.

OBJECTS:
- Crate (brown): a U-shaped / bowl-shaped cluster of brown cells. Origin starts
  at Position (3,1). Has a numeric field addWeight (starts at 0). The brown cells
  form the two side walls (columns at origin.x-2 and origin.x+2, rows -1..1) plus
  a bottom row across the middle (row +1). It forms an open-top container.
- Rock (silver): a single silver cell. None exist at start; rocks are created by
  clicking (see below). Rocks fall under gravity.
- Water (blue): a body of water filling the lower region. Initialized as a blue
  rectangle covering columns 0..GRID_SIZE over rows 3..GRID_SIZE (the bottom of
  the grid). Behaves as a liquid each step.

PLAYER ACTIONS:
- left / right / up / down: NO HANDLERS. There are no `on left/right/up/down`
  rules, so the arrow/move actions are no-ops.
- noop: no-op (does nothing).
- click: THE ONLY meaningful action, and CLICK LOCATION MATTERS. On click, if the
  clicked cell is a free position (isFreePos), a new silver Rock is spawned at the
  clicked (x,y). If the position is not free, nothing happens.

PASSIVE / CLOCK DYNAMICS (every step):
- Gravity on rocks (nextRock): each rock tries to move down one cell. It moves
  only if the cell below is within bounds AND not occupied by another rock or by
  the crate's wall/floor cells. Otherwise it stays. So rocks fall and settle on
  top of the crate floor, other rocks, or the grid bottom.
- Water (nextLiquid): the water updates as a liquid each step. If a water cell's
  position coincides with a rock's position, that water cell moves up (displaced);
  otherwise it follows normal liquid flow (nextLiquid). Rocks displace water.

CRATE WEIGHT / SINKING (nextCrate):
- Each step, count rocks currently inside the crate region (inCrate): a rock is
  "in" if its x is within [origin.x-2, origin.x+2] and its y is within [0,
  origin.y] (the crate's interior column/row span).
- If the crate's current addWeight < (number of rocks inside) AND that count < 5,
  then addWeight increments by 1 and the whole crate moves down one cell
  (moveDown). Otherwise the crate is unchanged.
- Effect: dropping rocks into the crate makes it progressively sink one row per
  added rock. moveDown has no lower-bound check, but the rule freezes once the
  count of rocks inside reaches 5 (the weight < 5 gate). So the crate sinks at
  most 4 rows — coming to rest with its floor on the bottom grid row (row 6) —
  and then never moves again. (Verified in the recorded run: the crate settles
  at rows 4-6 with its floor on row 6 and stays put for the rest of the episode.)

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS:
- Rocks block each other and rest on the crate floor/walls.
- Rocks displace water cells upward.
- Rocks inside the crate add weight and sink it.

COLOR CHANGES: none — objects keep their fixed colors (brown crate, silver rocks,
blue water).

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD: none defined. There is no win condition, goal object,
or reward rule in the SEXP. The game is a free-running sandbox: drop rocks into
the crate (via clicks) and watch it sink while water reacts.
