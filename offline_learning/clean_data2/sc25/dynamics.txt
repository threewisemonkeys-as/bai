sc25 (game_id sc25-635fd71a) — game mechanics

OVERVIEW
- A top-down "spellcasting maze". You steer a wizard (the player sprite) through
  a walled maze and out an exit portal to advance to the next level. Most exits
  are blocked by geometry or obstacles that can only be cleared by CASTING A
  SPELL. A spell is cast by drawing its 3x3 glyph on a clickable pad at the
  bottom of the screen. There are 6 levels; levels 1-3 each teach one spell,
  levels 4-6 combine them.
- Reaching the exit gives reward +0.1 and loads the next level. Clearing level 6
  wins. Spending too many actions in a level loses it.

GRID & LAYERS
- One 64x64 grid layer (a single <grid_0>), 16-color palette (index 0..15).
- Coordinate convention used below and on the CLI: (X = column, Y = row), X
  grows rightward, Y grows downward. A CLI `click_X_Y` -> ACTION6 with x=X, y=Y.

PALETTE (index -> rendered color -> meaning)
- 5  = black  : maze WALLS (the level's `duvwsv-*` sprite; fills the screen,
                with transparent holes where corridors are). Player cannot enter.
- 2  = gray   : walkable CORRIDOR / room floor; also a 3x3 pad cell that is OFF.
- 0  = white  : a 3x3 pad cell that is part of the level's TARGET glyph (a hint
                of which cells you must fill). Off-but-target cells render white.
- 3  = dark gray : border frame of the bottom 3x3 pad panel (and some obstacles).
- 9 / 10 = blue / light-blue : the PLAYER wizard (`pluyoo`) AND the EXIT portal
                (`exydhv`). Both use these two colors; the exit is a hollow 5x5
                framed square, the player a filled 2x2 block (scaled).
- 14 = green  : the BUDGET METER, a vertical bar at the far-right columns
                (x=62-63). It fills toward black as you spend actions.
- 15 = magenta: the spell-selector icon at the bottom-left, and the lit color of
                a filled pad cell when the selected/required spell is
                sieesc_chwjgc.
- 11 = lit color of a filled pad cell for spell tevyeq.
- 6  = lit color of a filled pad cell for spell fibcey; also the color of a
       `tagsmh` destructible-target box (and the fibcey fireball flickers 6/7).
- 13 = a `dosorb` obstacle block (a wall segment that a fireball can clear).
- 8  = pad cells flash this color during the cast wind-up animation.
Only the final settled frame of any multi-frame cast/teleport/slide animation is
rendered.

THE PLAYER
- `pluyoo` wizard. Has a position, a facing direction (0=up,1=down,2=left,
  3=right) and a SCALE of 1 (small, 2x2 px) or 2 (big, 4x4 px). Starts at scale 2
  in levels 1-3.
- Facing controls the aim of the fibcey fireball.

PLAYER ACTIONS (movement) — ACTION1/2/3/4
- ACTION1 = move UP   (sets facing=up).
- ACTION2 = move DOWN (sets facing=down).
- ACTION3 = move LEFT (sets facing=left).
- ACTION4 = move RIGHT(sets facing=right).
- Step size is 4 px when player scale=2, 2 px when scale=1.
- Movement is collision-checked: the player will not pass through walls (color 5)
  or solid obstacles. IMPORTANT: a big (scale-2) player is 4x4 px and cannot fit
  through a 1-cell/narrow gap; a small (scale-1) player can. So narrow passages
  require the shrink spell first.
- Even when a move is BLOCKED (no translation), it still (a) updates the player's
  facing/rotation — a visible change — and (b) costs one action from the budget.
- Walking the player onto/into the exit portal `exydhv` triggers a short slide
  animation and then advances to the next level.

CLICK (ACTION6) — the 3x3 spell pad
- A 3x3 pad sits at the bottom-center. Its nine cells map to display-click
  coordinates as: x = 25 + 5*col, y = 50 + 5*row, for row,col in {0,1,2}:
        (r0,c0)=click_25_50  (r0,c1)=click_30_50  (r0,c2)=click_35_50
        (r1,c0)=click_25_55  (r1,c1)=click_30_55  (r1,c2)=click_35_55
        (r2,c0)=click_25_60  (r2,c1)=click_30_60  (r2,c2)=click_35_60
- Clicking a pad cell TOGGLES it (off->on or on->off). Off cells render gray
  (or white if they belong to the target glyph); on cells render in the selected
  spell's lit color.
- A click that lands elsewhere (a spell-selector icon, empty space, a wall) does
  not toggle the pad; clicking the currently-selected spell icon replays its
  demo animation. Only pad-cell clicks matter for casting.

SPELLS — the three 3x3 glyphs (row-major, X=filled)
  tevyeq          sieesc_chwjgc        fibcey
  X X .           . X .                . X .
  . X .           X . X                . X .
  . . .           . X .                . X .
  cells/clicks:
  - tevyeq        = (0,0),(0,1),(1,1) -> click_25_50, click_30_50, click_30_55
  - sieesc_chwjgc = (0,1),(1,0),(1,2),(2,1)
                    -> click_30_50, click_25_55, click_35_55, click_30_60
  - fibcey        = (0,1),(1,1),(2,1) -> click_30_50, click_30_55, click_30_60

- A spell fires the instant the pad EXACTLY matches a glyph that is ALLOWED in
  the current level (level data field `efvw`). On firing, a cast animation plays
  and the pad is cleared back to all-off.
- If you draw a valid glyph that is NOT allowed in this level (e.g. the fibcey
  line while in level 1, which only allows sieesc_chwjgc), NOTHING happens and
  the filled cells simply stay lit until you toggle them off. This lets you build
  up to one glyph by passing through another.

SPELL EFFECTS
- sieesc_chwjgc (the plus/diamond) = RESIZE. Toggles the player between scale 2
  and scale 1. Shrinking lets the wizard fit narrow gaps. Growing only succeeds
  if there is room; if blocked it just flashes red and stays small. (Growing into
  a `enjehv-pahtoz` pickup removes it and refunds 10 budget — seen in later
  levels.)
- tevyeq (the corner L) = TELEPORT. Warps the player to the next `tevyeq-tagsmh`
  beacon in the level, cycling through them on repeated casts. (Level 2 has one
  beacon up near its exit.)
- fibcey (the vertical line) = FIREBALL. Launches a projectile in the player's
  current FACING direction. It flies until it hits something; if it strikes a
  `tagsmh` target box it destroys that box AND all `dosorb` obstacle blocks in
  the level, opening paths. So: face the target, then draw fibcey.

ACTION BUDGET / METER / LOSS
- Each level has an action budget `slfh` (observed: L1=50, L2=25, L3=50, L4=35,
  L5=65, L6=60). Every movement action AND every pad-cell toggle spends 1. A
  spell-completing click spends 2 (the toggle plus the cast tick).
- The green meter at the right edge fills (turns black) in proportion to budget
  spent. If spent count exceeds the budget, you LOSE the level.

AVAILABLE-ACTION TRANSITIONS
- In every observed state the available actions are exactly
  [ACTION1, ACTION2, ACTION3, ACTION4, ACTION6]. They do not change by state.
  (Movement may be a no-op against a wall, and a click may be a no-op off the
  pad, but the actions are always offered.)

LEVEL / GOAL STRUCTURE
- Level 1 (allows sieesc_chwjgc): wizard starts big in a horizontal corridor; the
  exit is to the left behind a narrow gap. SHRINK (sieesc) to fit through, walk
  left into the exit.
- Level 2 (allows tevyeq): exit is up past a barrier. TELEPORT (tevyeq) to the
  beacon near the top, then walk up into the exit.
- Level 3 (allows fibcey): a `dosorb` wall blocks the corridor down to the exit;
  a `tagsmh` target sits to the right. Face right, FIREBALL (fibcey) the target
  to clear the dosorb, then walk down/left into the exit.
- Levels 4-6 allow multiple spells (combinations of the above) and are larger.

TUTORIAL QUIRK (level 1 only)
- The FIRST action taken after reset in level 1 is consumed by a demo: it blinks
  the required glyph (sieesc_chwjgc) on the pad, then returns. The player does NOT
  move and the budget is NOT charged for that first action. From the second
  action on, the game behaves normally.

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD
- Reward +0.1 per level cleared (entering an exit). No other reward signal.
- Losing fires when the per-level action budget is exceeded.
- The game has 6 levels (win_levels=6); clearing level 6 wins.
