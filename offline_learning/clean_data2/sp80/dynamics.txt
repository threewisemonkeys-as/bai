sp80 (sp80-589a99af) — game mechanics

Genre: a "water-pour / plumbing" puzzle. You reposition movable bars during a
quiet "change" phase, then release a water spill (ACTION5). Water pours from a
fixed faucet, is split/deflected by the bars, and must land in EVERY bucket
without any water reaching the floor. Solve a level to advance; there are 6
levels (win_levels=6).

GRID & LAYERS
- One <grid_0> layer, rendered 64x64 (16-color palette). The play area is a
  16x16 board (levels 1-3) or 20x20 board (levels 4-6), scaled to fill 64x64:
  each board cell is 4x4 display pixels for a 16-board (64/16), 3.2px for a
  20-board. A 1-cell gray frame (color 1) borders the board; the bottom rows
  are a gray floor line (color 1).
- Top display row 0 is an INTERFACE bar, not board content: a horizontal
  green/black meter (color 14 = filled, 0 = empty) that shows the remaining
  step budget for the current level. It shrinks left-to-right as steps are
  spent. Ignore row 0 when reading board geometry.

PALETTE / OBJECTS (index -> observed render color)
- 12 background (orange). 1 frame + floor (gray).
- 4  FAUCET (sowlljgtjvn), dark. A small source marker fixed near the top edge;
  water spawns from the cell just below it. Levels can have 1-3 faucets.
- 6  WATER drop (liolfvkveqg), magenta. A single static "primed" drop usually
  sits directly under each faucet at rest; the live flow is transient (see
  SPILL — it resolves inside one step and is not observed frame-by-frame).
- 8  BAR, DESELECTED (plzwjbfyfli), red. A horizontal bar 3-8 cells wide; the
  movable/ splitter piece(s). Some bars embed a color-4 secondary faucet cell.
- 9  BAR, SELECTED, blue. Exactly one piece is "selected" at a time; movement
  actions move the selected piece. Selecting recolors it 8->9; deselecting 9->8.
- 11 BUCKET (repwkzbkhxl), yellow. A cup shape (3 wide, 2 tall) with an OPEN
  top-center cell: cells = two top corners + full bottom row, center-top empty.
  Water must enter the open center. ALL buckets must be filled to clear a level.
- 13 FILLED BUCKET (transient during the spill resolution).
- 15 RAMP (tuvkdkhdokr), an L/corner piece that deflects water 90 degrees;
  also movable/selectable (levels 5-6).
- 14 overflow/marker flash + the top step meter.

PLAYER ACTIONS (all six are always offered: ACTION1..ACTION6)
The game has two internal phases, "change" (interactive, default) and "spill"
(the water cascade). All player input happens in "change"; ACTION5 kicks off
"spill" which then resolves on its own. Every change-phase action spends 1 step
from the level's step budget (the top meter ticks down).

Movement of the SELECTED piece (UNROTATED levels, level 1 & 4; dojfslwbg=0):
- ACTION1 : move selected piece UP    (row - 1)
- ACTION2 : move selected piece DOWN  (row + 1)
- ACTION3 : move selected piece LEFT  (col - 1)
- ACTION4 : move selected piece RIGHT (col + 1)
  A move is blocked (no-op, but still spends a step) if it would put the piece
  above row 3 (y<3) or overlap/abut a bucket. Pieces may overlap each other.
- ACTION5 : RELEASE THE WATER (start the spill). See SPILL.
- ACTION6 : CLICK. Selects the bar/ramp piece under the cursor as the new
  selected piece (recolor 8/15 -> 9). Click on empty space does nothing.
  Coordinate convention: click_X_Y on the CLI -> ACTION6 x=X y=Y, where
  X = display COLUMN, Y = display ROW in the 64x64 frame. To target board cell
  (col,row) on a 16-board use display x≈col*4+2, y≈row*4+2 (e.g. the level-1
  bar cell (col5,row4) -> click_22_18).

ROTATED LEVELS (gotcha): levels 2,3,5,6 set dojfslwbg=180 (or other), which
rotates the ENTIRE rendered board 180 degrees (faucet appears at the BOTTOM,
buckets at the TOP, bars upside-down) AND remaps the movement actions and the
click x/y so that, in screen terms, ACTION1/ACTION2 and ACTION3/ACTION4 swap.
The underlying physics is identical to the unrotated description; only the
on-screen direction of each action label changes. Level 1 and level 4 are
unrotated and read literally.

SPILL (ACTION5) — resolves entirely within ONE env step
- Pressing ACTION5 in "change" phase deselects the current piece and starts the
  spill. The engine then runs the water cascade to completion BEFORE the step
  returns, so the flowing water is NOT visible frame-by-frame — only the final
  settled outcome is rendered.
- Water spawns under each faucet and falls DOWN (in board coordinates). On the
  way down:
  * Hitting the TOP of a BAR (plzwjbfyfli): the stream splits and walks
    OUTWARD along the bar, one cell per tick, dropping off BOTH ENDS of the
    bar. So a width-W bar centered over the faucet sends one stream off its
    left end and one off its right end — moving the bar horizontally aims those
    two fall points. (This is the core puzzle lever.)
  * Reaching a BUCKET's open top-center: that bucket FILLS (turns 13, counted).
  * Hitting a RAMP (tuvkdkhdokr): the stream is deflected 90 degrees.
  * Reaching the FLOOR (color 1 line): OVERFLOW — marks failure for this spill.

WIN / LOSE / REWARD
- After the cascade settles: if EVERY bucket is filled AND there was NO overflow
  to the floor, the level is solved -> advance to the next level (reward +0.1,
  fresh layout & step budget). The rendered frame after ACTION5 then shows the
  NEXT level.
- Otherwise the spill "failed": the level resets to its starting layout (minus
  the steps already spent) for another attempt. You get up to 4 spill attempts
  per level; a 5th ACTION5 (after 4 failed spills) loses the game (term).
- Running the step budget to 0 (top meter empties) also loses. Level step
  budgets: L1=30, L2=45, L3=100, L4=120, L5/L6=100/120.
- Solving all 6 levels wins the game.

LEVEL 1 (unrotated; the clean-data level)
- One faucet at board (col9,row0); one primed water drop below it at (col9,row1).
- One movable bar, plzwjbfyfli-5 (5 wide), starting at board cols 3..7, row 4,
  selected (blue) at reset.
- Two buckets, openings at board col 5 and col 11 (faucet sits between them at
  col 9). Floor at the bottom.
- SOLUTION: the 5-wide bar must straddle the faucet so its left end drops water
  into the col-5 opening and its right end into the col-11 opening. A 5-wide bar
  at cols 6..10 routes its ends to col5 and col11. From the start (cols 3..7)
  move RIGHT 3 (to cols 6..10) then ACTION5 -> both buckets fill, no overflow ->
  win (reward +0.1, advance to level 2). Moving the bar's row does not matter as
  long as it still covers the faucet column and sits above the buckets.

NOTES / CAVEATS
- The live water flow is internal to the ACTION5 step; the only observable spill
  effects are (a) win -> the next level's layout appears, or (b) fail -> the same
  level layout reappears (one fewer attempt). Repeated ACTION5 with a wrong bar
  position just cycles attempts until the 4-attempt loss.
- The Reward column in trajectory.csv is recorded per observation row and reads
  0.0 on the settled win frame; the +0.1 win signal is confirmed via the live
  driver (it fires on the ACTION5 transition that produces the level change).
