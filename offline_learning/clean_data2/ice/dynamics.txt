ICE — game mechanics

Grid: 16 x 16.

OBJECTS
- CelestialBody ("sun"): a 2x2 block at the top-left corner, cells (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1).
  Has a Bool field `day`. Colored "gold" when day=true, "gray" when day=false.
  Starts as day=true (gold). Position is fixed; it never moves.
- Cloud: a horizontal 3-cell gray bar. Internally spans relative cells (-1,0),(0,0),(1,0)
  with origin initialized at (4,0), so it occupies columns 3,4,5 on row 0 at the start.
- Water: a list, initially empty. Each Water drop is a single cell with a Bool field `liquid`.
  Colored "blue" when liquid=true, "lightblue" when liquid=false (frozen / ice).

PLAYER ACTIONS
- left:  moves the cloud one cell left,  if the moved cloud stays within bounds (else no change).
- right: moves the cloud one cell right, if the moved cloud stays within bounds (else no change).
  The cloud clamps at the edges: leftmost occupies columns 0-2, rightmost columns 13-15.
- down: spawns one new Water drop just below the cloud's origin, at (cloud_origin_x, 1).
  The new drop's `liquid` flag is set to the sun's current `day` value:
  day (gold sun) -> blue liquid drop; night (gray sun) -> lightblue solid drop.
- click ROW COL: a click WITH a coordinate toggles global day/night state — flips the sun's
  `day` (gold<->gray) AND flips the `liquid` flag of every existing water drop
  (blue<->lightblue, both directions, melt/freeze all at once).
  The click LOCATION does not matter; a click on ANY cell has the same global effect.
  A coordinate is REQUIRED: a bare `click` with no ROW/COL is rejected by the action parser
  and does NOTHING (no-op — the sun and drops are unchanged).
- up: NO handler — no-op (does nothing).
- noop: does nothing.

PASSIVE / PER-STEP DYNAMICS (every tick)
- The sun and the cloud do NOT move on their own; they only move via the actions above.
- Every water drop updates each tick based on its own `liquid` flag:
  - Solid (lightblue) drops: fall straight down one cell if the cell directly below is free
    and within bounds; otherwise they stay put. They do not move sideways.
  - Liquid (blue) drops: fall straight down one cell if the cell below is free; if blocked,
    they scan the entire next row down for open "holes" (a free cell whose cell above is also
    free), pick the nearest one, and step ONE cell per tick toward it (water-like settling /
    sideways spreading along the floor), stopping when no valid move exists or at the bottom row.
- With no water present, a plain step leaves the board unchanged.

SPAWN / DESPAWN
- Water is only created by the `down` action (one drop per press). Drops are never removed;
  they accumulate and pile up.

COLOR CHANGES
- Sun: gold (day) <-> gray (night), toggled only by click.
- Water: blue (liquid) <-> lightblue (solid), set at spawn by current day flag and flipped
  for all drops by click. No automatic/threshold-based color changes.

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS
- Falling water cannot enter occupied cells; drops stack and pile on each other and at the
  floor. The cloud passes through nothing (it lives on row 0); spawned water appears below it.

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD
- None. There is no goal cell, no score, no win/lose or termination condition in the program.
