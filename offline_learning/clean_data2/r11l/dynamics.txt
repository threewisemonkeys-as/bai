r11l (game_id r11l-495a7899) — game mechanics

OVERVIEW
- A click-only "drag the colored objects onto their matching colored targets"
  puzzle. The agent never moves a player avatar directly; instead it SELECTS a
  small handle and then MOVES that handle by clicking an empty cell. Each color
  group has several handles; the group's "anchor" sits at the CENTROID of its
  handles. A level is solved when every color group's anchor overlaps its
  same-colored target zone. Solving a level advances to the next one (+0.1
  reward). There are 6 levels (win_levels=6).

GRID & LAYERS
- 64 x 64 grid, single rendered grid layer per observation (layers=1).
- 16-color palette. Background/letterbox color index = 5 (renders near-black).
- Coordinate convention (CLI -> engine): click_X_Y  ->  ACTION6 x=X y=Y, with
  x = COLUMN, y = ROW (origin top-left, x grows right, y grows down). Verified
  empirically: clicking x=39 y=20 lands on the Level-1 target at col~39,row~20.

ONLY ACTION: ACTION6 (click). available_actions is ALWAYS exactly ['ACTION6']
  at reset and in every state (it never changes). x,y are quantized to a 16x16
  lattice of clickable points (multiples of 4 in 0..63) by the engine's action
  set, but any in-grid click is mapped to a grid cell.

OBJECTS / SPRITE FAMILIES (decoded from source)
- wakneh-*      : WALLS (collidable border / obstacles). Render gray. A move is
                  BLOCKED if the selected handle would collide with a wall.
- roefwulewcui-*: HANDLES — the clickable, draggable pieces. One color group has
                  2-3 handles. Each handle is color-remapped (0->3); the
                  CURRENTLY SELECTED handle is highlighted (remap 3->0, renders
                  bright white). Thin connector lines are drawn from the group
                  anchor to each of its handles (a rendered UI overlay).
- roefwu-*      : ANCHOR — one per color group. It is auto-repositioned to the
                  CENTROID of that group's handles after every move. This is the
                  object that must reach the goal (filled colored blob).
- flkdtg-*      : GOAL / target zone for the same-suffix color group (dashed
                  colored square outline). The anchor must overlap this.
- defgjl-*      : DANGER terrain (large cyan/light-blue region, pixel value 10).
                  If a group anchor finishes a move overlapping defgjl it counts
                  as a hit; 5 hits -> lose() (see WIN/LOSE). Levels 1 has none;
                  Level 2+ have it.
- puukul-* / hawffu- / flkdtg-dirwzt / "whkxtx" groups : extra mechanics that
  only appear from Level 4+ (paint pickups that recolor "whkxtx" anchors, and
  "dirwzt" decoy groups that are EXCLUDED from the win check). Not exercised by
  the train/test trajectories here (which stay within Levels 1-2).

CLICK SEMANTICS (the single action, two outcomes)
The ACTION6 handler converts the click to a grid cell (col,row), then:
  1) SELECT — if the clicked cell lies inside the bounding box of any handle
     (roefwulewcui-*), that handle becomes the selected handle (bright-white
     highlight; previous selection reverts to its group color). The action then
     completes; NOTHING moves. NOTE: because select is checked first, clicking a
     cell that is occupied by a handle can NEVER move a handle there — it just
     (re)selects that handle. To move onto/near a goal you must click an EMPTY
     cell.
  2) MOVE — else, if there is a selected handle, the handle is moved so that its
     CENTER lands on the clicked cell (target = click minus handle half-size),
     PROVIDED the new position does not collide with a wall (wakneh). If it would
     hit a wall the click is rejected (no move). On a successful move the group's
     anchor is recomputed to the new handle centroid (a brief blink/flash sprite
     plays when an anchor snaps onto its goal).
  3) Otherwise the action is a no-op.

AUTO-SELECTION AT LEVEL START
- On entering a level, all handles are collected and sorted by distance from the
  origin (x^2+y^2); the closest handle is pre-selected automatically. (E.g. in
  Level 1 the top-left handle starts selected/white.)

ANCHOR = CENTROID RULE (key mechanic)
- A group's anchor is always placed at the average center of that group's
  handles. With 2 handles you bracket the goal (put one handle on each side so
  their midpoint is the goal); with 3 handles the anchor is their centroid.
- Consequence (verified): you generally CANNOT solve a 2-handle group by stacking
  both handles on the goal cell, because the second click would land on the first
  handle and merely re-select it. Place the two handles at two DISTINCT cells
  whose midpoint is the goal.

LEVELS
- Level 1: one color group ("pumlzd"), 2 handles, 1 goal, no danger terrain.
  Anchor must overlap the single dashed goal square (~col 39, row 20).
- Level 2: two color groups ("orrqlj" orange anchor top-left -> goal bottom;
  "pumlzd" purple anchor center-right -> goal upper-right) PLUS a large cyan
  defgjl danger region through the middle. Both anchors must reach their goals.
- Levels 3-6 add more groups, walls, danger terrain, paint pickups, and decoy
  ("dirwzt") groups. Not covered here.

WIN / LOSE / SCORE / REWARD
- LEVEL SOLVED: after a move settles, if EVERY non-"dirwzt" group's anchor
  collides with its same-color goal, the engine calls next_level(); reward +0.1
  is delivered on that step and play continues on the next level (term stays
  False until the final level). Empirically Level 1 -> Level 2 gives reward=0.1.
- LOSE: the action budget is 60 (_max_actions). When _action_count reaches 60
  the game calls lose() and ends. Also, if a group anchor ends a move overlapping
  the danger terrain (defgjl) 5 times (yledlprvvkb counter), the game calls
  lose().
- No per-step reward other than the +0.1 level-completion bonus.

CAVEATS
- Moves are essentially instantaneous (interpolation length havofgepjpl=1); only
  the settled frame is observed.
- The bright-white piece in any frame is the currently-selected handle, not a
  special object.
- defgjl danger triggers a loss only on the 5th overlapping settle, so a single
  stray move near the cyan region does not end the game.
