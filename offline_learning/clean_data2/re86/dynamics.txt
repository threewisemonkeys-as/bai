re86 (re86-8af5384d) — game mechanics

OVERVIEW
- A "crosshair alignment" puzzle. The board holds one or more movable, single-
  colored PLUS ("+") shaped crosses whose arms span (nearly) the whole grid.
  Scattered around are small colored TARGET dots, one set of dots per cross
  color. You drive the crosses so that, for each color, that cross's two arms
  (one full row + one full column) pass through ALL of that color's target dots
  simultaneously. When every target is covered the level is solved.
- There are 8 levels of increasing complexity. The clean-data trajectories here
  cover Level 1 (the simplest: two crosses, no walls, no recolor boxes).

GRID & LAYERS
- 64 x 64 grid, 16-color palette (indices 0..15). One grid layer per frame.
- BACKGROUND_COLOR = 5 (the empty board; rendered dark).
- PADDING_COLOR = 3 (camera letterbox).
- ROW 63 is a STEP-COUNTER progress bar, NOT part of the puzzle: cells set to
  color 15 = steps remaining, color 1 = steps spent. The bar shrinks left-to-
  right by one notch's worth of fraction as the budget is consumed, and refills
  when a new level loads.
- Coordinates here are (x = col, y = row), x grows right, y grows down. NOTE:
  the ASCII probe's rendered columns can sit ~1 col off from the source's
  internal sprite coordinates (a camera offset); the internal coords are what
  the win check uses. There is NO click action, so the click x/y convention
  does not apply to this game.

OBJECTS / PALETTE
- Movable CROSS (sprite tag "0031cppcuvqlbi"): a "+"-shaped sprite of a single
  color, made of one full vertical bar and one full horizontal bar crossing at
  the sprite's center. Level 1 has two: a color-11 cross (rendered yellow) and a
  color-9 cross (rendered blue).
  * The ACTIVE (currently-controlled) cross has its CENTER cell painted color 0
    (rendered as a small dark dot at the intersection). An INACTIVE cross shows
    its own color at the center. Exactly one cross is active at a time. This dot
    is the reliable "which cross am I driving" indicator.
- TARGET dots (goal sprite, tag "0054xnsuqceejm"): a full-grid overlay drawn as
  several small 3x3 boxes — a border of color 4 around a single center cell
  whose color is a target color (9 or 11 in Level 1). Only the colored CENTER
  cell matters to the win check; the color-4 border and the surrounding -1 are
  ignored. Visually, each target is a colored dot inside a faint square. When a
  cross arm slides over a target, the arm's color overwrites the dot, so a
  covered target visually "disappears" under the arm — a handy progress cue.
- (Levels >= 4, seen but not exercised here) WALLS (tag "0003dlchiwseii"):
  ring/diamond obstacle sprites that block cross movement. COLOR BOXES (tag
  "0007dtbisvazhv"): 6x6 squares (color-2 border, colored interior) that
  RECOLOR a cross which collides with them via a multi-step flood-fill cascade
  (driven by the game's per-step step() handler over several frames). Some
  higher levels also start crosses rotated 90/180 deg. None of this occurs in
  Level 1.

ACTIONS (available_actions = [1,2,3,4,5]; there is NO ACTION6 / click, ever)
- Movement step size is 3 pixels. Each action below also decrements the step
  budget by 1.
- ACTION1 = move the ACTIVE cross UP    by 3  (dy = -3).
- ACTION2 = move the ACTIVE cross DOWN  by 3  (dy = +3).
- ACTION3 = move the ACTIVE cross LEFT  by 3  (dx = -3).
- ACTION4 = move the ACTIVE cross RIGHT by 3  (dx = +3).
  * A move is rejected (no-op) if it would carry the active cross's CENTER out
    of the 64x64 camera, and (higher levels) if it would collide with a wall.
    In Level 1 there are no walls, so movement is free within bounds.
- ACTION5 = CYCLE which cross is active: advance to the next movable sprite in
  level order (wrapping from the last back to the first). The previously active
  cross's center reverts to its own color; the newly active cross's center is
  set to color 0. With two crosses, ACTION5 simply toggles between them.

AVAILABLE-ACTION TRANSITIONS
- The available action set is the constant {ACTION1, ACTION2, ACTION3, ACTION4,
  ACTION5} on every step of every level (confirmed empirically). Availability
  does not change with state. Only emit actions from this set.

WIN / LEVEL / SCORE / GAME-OVER
- After every action the game composites all movable crosses onto a blank canvas
  and checks the goal overlay: for each goal center cell (the colored dots), the
  composited canvas color at that cell must equal the goal color. If ALL goal
  centers match -> next_level() fires.
- Geometry of a solve: a single "+" covers exactly one full row and one full
  column. To cover 4 same-colored dots, two of them must lie on the cross's
  chosen column and the other two on its chosen row; the cross center then goes
  to that unique (row, col) intersection. In Level 1:
    * Yellow (color 11) cross: targets at internal (col,row) (15,3),(6,9),
      (24,9),(15,17)  ->  center must reach (col 15, row 9).
      It starts at center (col 21, row 27); solve = 2x LEFT + 6x UP.
    * Blue (color 9) cross: targets at (48,16),(40,24),(54,24),(48,35)  ->
      center must reach (col 48, row 24).
      It starts at center (col 36, row 45); solve = 4x RIGHT + 7x UP.
  Both deltas are multiples of the 3px step, so exact alignment is reachable.
- Completing a level yields reward +0.1 and loads the next level, whose step
  counter is reset to that level's budget: L1=100, L2=100, L3=200, L4=200,
  L5=250, L6=200, L7=300, L8=400. The episode is an 8-level sequence
  (win_levels=8); solving Level 1 keeps term=False (more levels remain).
- GAME OVER: if the step-counter budget reaches 0 before the level is solved,
  the game calls lose() (game over). No other termination/penalty.

SEED-0 / LEVEL-1 START STATE
- Two crosses: blue (color 9) is ACTIVE at reset (center dot at internal
  col 36,row 45); yellow (color 11) is inactive (center col 21,row 27).
- ACTION1-4 drive whichever cross is active; ACTION5 toggles active blue<->yellow.
