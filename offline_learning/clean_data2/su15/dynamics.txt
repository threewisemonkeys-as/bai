su15 (su15-1944f8ab) — game mechanics

OVERVIEW
- A "2048"-style merge-and-deliver puzzle driven entirely by a click "pulse".
- You never move a piece directly. ACTION6 clicks a cell; that emits an
  expanding circular pulse that PULLS nearby numbered blocks toward the click
  point. Two same-level blocks that get pulled together MERGE into the next
  block in the chain. The per-level goal is to assemble the required block
  level and park it on the blue goal zone. ACTION7 UNDOes the last action.
- 9 levels. Completing a level gives reward +0.1 (≈1/9) and advances; the
  episode terminates (full win) only after level 9. win_levels=9.

GRID & LAYERS
- One 64x64 grid layer (one <grid_0> block), 16-color palette (index 0..15).
- Coordinates are (x=col, y=row); x grows right, y grows down.
  CLI convention: click_X_Y -> ACTION6 x=X y=Y, with x=column, y=row.
  Display coords map 1:1 to grid coords (identity).
- Row layout (constant):
    rows 0..9   : top header band (color 4, dark gray). Holds the TARGET
                  indicator: a swatch of the block level you must deliver this
                  level, plus (from L2 on) a small multi-color palette legend
                  in the top-left corner. NOT clickable.
    rows 10..62 : the play field. Background is color 5 (renders near-black).
    row 63      : the STEP-COUNTER bar — a run of color-0 cells whose length is
                  proportional to remaining steps for this level. Shrinks as you
                  act; reaching 0 loses. NOT clickable.
- Clicks with y<=9 or y>=63 are ignored (axaxyjxqoe early-returns).

PALETTE / OBJECTS
- Numbered blocks (the merge chain; tag "zmlxwcvwb"). Each level = a filled
  square sprite, bigger and a different color as the level rises:
    level 0 : 1x1  color 10 (cyan dot)
    level 1 : 2x2  color  6 (magenta)
    level 2 : 3x3  color 15 (purple)
    level 3 : 4x4  color 11 (yellow)
    level 4 : 5x5  color 12
    level 5 : 7x7  color  8
    level 6 : 8x8  color  9
    level 7 : 9x9  color  7
    level 8 : 10x10 color 14   (top of chain)
- Goal zone(s) (tag "xkstxyqbs"): a fuzzy blue blob, color 9 (e.g. a 9x9 disk).
  This is the delivery target.
- Enemies (tag "ybnveypak"): spiky star-shaped sprites. Absent in levels 1-3;
  first appear in level 4. They chase the nearest block each step and, on
  contact, DECREMENT that block's level (degrade your work). The click pulse
  pushes them away. (Not exercised in the train/test trajectories, which cover
  levels 1-2 only.)
- Decorations seen on L1: a dotted color-3 diagonal "hint" trail from the
  starting block to the goal, and a white "+" hint marker (color 0, an
  "ooutlqdaq" sprite) that is simply removed when the pulse passes over it.

AVAILABLE ACTIONS
- Always exactly [ACTION6, ACTION7] in every state of every level (verified —
  the available set never changes). ACTION6 carries x,y; ACTION7 takes none.

ACTION6 — CLICK / PULSE  (the only way to move pieces)
- Emits an expanding circular pulse centered on the clicked cell, radius 8
  (kacsjmxae=8), animated over 4 sub-steps (gdamdvokm=4). A bright color-0 ring
  is drawn in an overlay during the animation; only the settled frame is shown.
- GRAB: every numbered block whose body lies within radius 8 of the click is
  grabbed and pulled TOWARD the click point, up to 4 cells per sub-step, and
  stops once it reaches the click. Net effect of one click: a block within ~8
  cells of the click ends up AT (or on top of) the clicked cell.
- MERGE: after the pull, any two grabbed blocks of the SAME level that overlap
  fuse into ONE block of the next level, placed at their average position
  (cyan+cyan -> magenta 2x2, etc.). Two blocks of DIFFERENT levels that touch
  instead trigger a rejection flash and do NOT merge. Merging two level-8 blocks
  destroys them (top of chain).
- REPEL: enemies within the radius are pushed outward (away from the click).
- Therefore the two primitives are:
    move a block  : click ~7-8 cells from it in the direction you want it to go,
                    then leapfrog with successive clicks across the board;
    merge a pair  : click the midpoint between two same-level blocks so both are
                    pulled together and collide.
- Clicks outside rows 10..62 are ignored.

ACTION7 — UNDO
- Restores the state snapshot taken just before the last action (a history
  stack is pushed on each click). Reverts a move or a merge; repeating it
  unwinds further. Verified: undo after a merge splits the merged block back
  into its two source blocks at their prior positions.

LEVEL / GOAL / WIN STRUCTURE
- Each level has a data spec "xkstxyqbs" = required (block-level, count) — what
  must sit on the goal zone(s) — and a "steps" budget shown as the row-63 bar.
- A level completes when the required count of blocks of the required level have
  their CENTERS inside a goal zone. On completion: a short clear animation,
  reward +0.1, then auto-advance to the next level (term stays False until L9).
- Level 1 (steps=32, need 1x level-2 block): a single purple 3x3 block sits at
  bottom-left ~center (col4,row59); the blue goal is top-right (cols 44-52,
  rows 11-19, center ~(48,15)). No merging needed — just leapfrog-click the
  block up to the goal. (Empirically the reward fires the moment the block's
  center enters the goal box.)
- Level 2 (steps=32, need 1x level-3 block): eight scattered level-0 cyan dots
  and a central blue goal; you must merge the dots up the chain
  (0->1->2->3) and deliver the level-3 block. A central palette legend and a
  yellow (level-3) target swatch appear in the header.
- Later levels add more goals, multi-part specs, and enemies.

GAME OVER / SCORE / REWARD
- Reward: +0.1 on each level completion; 0.0 otherwise.
- Lose (term, no reward) if the step budget hits 0 or if all numbered blocks are
  removed. A normal click costs 1 step; a rejected (mismatched-level) merge
  costs more (escalating penalty).

CAVEATS
- A "level transition" here is NOT a terminal state — reward 0.1 fires and play
  continues into the next level within the same episode. Full termination only
  after level 9. The train/test trajectories intentionally cross the L1->L2
  boundary to show both the delivery and the merge mechanics.
- The pulse pulls blocks to the EXACT clicked cell (when in range), so block
  placement is precise; just keep each leapfrog click within ~8 cells of the
  block so it stays grabbed.
