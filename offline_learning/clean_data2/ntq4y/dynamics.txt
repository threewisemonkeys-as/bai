NTQ4Y — game mechanics

Grid: 16 x 16.

Coordinates below are written (col, row) = (x, y), origin top-left, y increasing
downward. NOTE the action/observation interface uses the OPPOSITE order: actions
are "click ROW COL" and the observation is indexed obs[row][col]. So e.g. the
vesselButton written below as (col 2, row 0) is selected by the action "click 0 2"
and appears at obs[0][2].

This is a "sandbox painter" with a water/liquid simulation. The player selects a
particle type from a top toolbar, then clicks empty cells to place that particle.
There is no goal or win condition.

Objects / colors / initial positions:
- Toolbar buttons (row y=0), each a single colored cell, fixed in place:
  - vesselButton: purple, at (col 2, row 0)  -> selects "vessel" particle
  - plugButton:   orange, at (col 5, row 0)  -> selects "plug" particle
  - waterButton:  blue,   at (col 8, row 0)  -> selects "water" particle
  - removeButton: gray,   at (col 11, row 0) -> removes ALL plugs
  - clearButton:  red,    at (col 14, row 0) -> clears ALL vessels, plugs, and water
- Vessel particles: purple cells. 12 placed initially, forming the left and right
  walls of an open-top funnel/V-shaped container (narrow at the bottom, flaring
  outward toward the top):
    left wall  (col,row): (6,15)(6,14)(6,13)(5,12)(4,11)(3,10)
    right wall (col,row): (9,15)(9,14)(9,13)(10,12)(11,11)(12,10)
  These act as immovable barrier walls.
- Plug particles: orange cells. 6 placed initially in a 2x3 block at the bottom
  center: (7,15)(8,15)(7,14)(8,14)(7,13)(8,13) — they seal the gap (cols 7,8)
  between the two vessel walls, so the container holds water until the plugs are
  removed.
- Water particles: blue cells. None placed initially; the player creates them.
- currentParticle: a string state, initial value "vessel". Determines what a
  free-cell click places.

Player actions:
- click: behavior depends on what was clicked.
  - Clicking a toolbar button cell triggers that button's handler (select a
    particle type, or remove all plugs, or clear everything). Click LOCATION
    matters (which button). The button cell is occupied, so a button click never
    also places a particle.
  - Clicking an empty/free cell (isFreePos) places ONE particle of the currently
    selected type (vessel / plug / water) at the clicked coordinates. Click
    location matters — the particle appears exactly where clicked.
  - Clicking an already-occupied, non-button cell does nothing (the isFreePos
    guard fails).
- left, right, up, down, noop: no action handlers exist — they cause no
  player-driven change. (The passive water simulation below still runs on these
  steps, since it fires every step.)

Passive / clock dynamics (on true, every step):
- Water flows every step via nextLiquid, applied to each water particle:
  - If the cell directly below the water is free (and the water is not already on
    the bottom row), the water moves straight down one cell.
  - Otherwise it looks at the row immediately below for the nearest reachable
    "hole" (a free cell with a free cell above it) and moves one step toward it
    (down/diagonally). If no such hole exists, it stays put.
  This produces gravity + sideways spreading: water falls and piles up, settling
  on top of and confined by the vessel walls and plugs. Removing the plugs opens
  the bottom of the container so any held water drains down to the floor.
- Vessels, plugs, and buttons never move and are unaffected by the simulation.

Spawning / despawning:
- Spawn: clicking a free cell adds one particle of the currently selected type.
- Despawn: removeButton deletes ALL plugs; clearButton deletes ALL vessels, plugs,
  and water. No automatic/periodic spawning or despawning.

Color changes: none — each object keeps its fixed color (vessels purple, plugs
orange, water blue; buttons purple/orange/blue/gray/red).

Collisions / interactions: a cell is occupied (not free) if it holds a vessel,
plug, water, or button. Water is confined by vessel cells, plug cells, other water,
and the row-0 buttons via the nextLiquid simulation; particle placement is blocked
on any occupied cell (isFreePos check).

Win / termination / reward: none. Open-ended sandbox; no score, goal, or end
state. Reward is always 0.
