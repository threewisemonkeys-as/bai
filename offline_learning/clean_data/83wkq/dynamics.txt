GAME: 83WKQ (Autumn DSL)

GRID
- 16x16 grid (GRID_SIZE = 16). Rows and cols are both indexed 0..15.

OBJECTS
- Particle: a single blue cell. The object is declared with a prototype
  cell at (0,0) "blue", but no particle exists in the world initially.
- particles: a list of Particle objects. It is initialized to the empty
  list, so the grid starts completely empty (no particles on screen).

PLAYER ACTIONS
- click ROW COL: adds a NEW blue particle at the clicked grid cell. Click
  LOCATION matters — the particle is created at exactly (ROW, COL) (the SEXP
  uses Position(click.x, click.y); the new particle is rendered at that
  cell). This is the ONLY way to introduce particles into the world.
  The newly spawned particle first appears AT the click cell (it does not
  move on its spawn frame); it begins random-walking on the next
  non-click frame.
- left, right, up, down: NO handlers exist for these. They do not move,
  add, or remove anything themselves. They are no-ops for game state — but
  the clock still ticks on these frames, so existing particles DO random-walk
  on a left/right/up/down frame (see CLOCK DYNAMICS).
- noop: same as the arrows — no handler of its own, but the clock still
  ticks so existing particles random-walk on a noop frame.

PASSIVE / CLOCK DYNAMICS
- On every clock tick that is NOT a click frame (i.e. noop or any arrow),
  EVERY existing particle moves to a uniformly random in-bounds orthogonally
  ADJACENT cell: uniformChoice over adjPositions(current origin).
- adjPositions returns only the IN-BOUNDS orthogonal neighbors (the 4
  candidates {(x-1,y),(x+1,y),(x,y-1),(x,y+1)} filtered to those with both
  coords in 0..15). So an interior particle has 4 choices, an edge particle
  3, and a corner particle 2. The current cell is NEVER a candidate, so on a
  non-click tick a particle ALWAYS moves — it can never stay in place.
- This is an independent random walk: each particle picks its own random
  step every non-click tick.

CLICK-FRAME MOVEMENT SUPPRESSION (important)
- On a tick where a click occurs, the `on clicked` handler reassigns the
  whole particles list (addObj over prev particles). This reassignment
  SUPERSEDES the next-update random walk for that frame, so on a click frame
  the EXISTING particles do NOT move — they hold their positions while the
  new particle is added at the click cell. Movement of existing particles
  resumes on the next non-click tick.
- Concretely: a particle moves on every noop/arrow frame and freezes on the
  frame in which any click is issued. (Verified: with a particle at (5,3),
  issuing a click leaves it at (5,3) that frame while a new particle appears
  at the click location; it then resumes walking.)

SPAWNING / DESPAWNING
- Spawning: only via click (one particle per click, at the click cell).
- Despawning: none. Particles are never removed; they persist and keep
  wandering for the rest of the episode. Particle count is monotonically
  non-decreasing and equals the number of clicks issued so far.

COLOR CHANGES
- None. Particles are always blue and never change color.

COLLISIONS / INTERACTIONS
- None. Particles do not interact with each other; multiple particles may
  occupy or pass through the same cell freely (no collision, no blocking).
  There are no walls and no object-to-object effects; the only boundary
  effect is that out-of-bounds neighbors are excluded from a particle's
  random-walk choices (see CLOCK DYNAMICS).

RENDERING NOTE
- The "blue(N)" legend count is the number of distinct blue CELLS, not the
  true particle count. Because particles may overlap (no collision), if two
  particles random-walk onto the same cell the legend count can read BELOW
  the true particle count. The true count only ever increases (one per
  click).

WIN / TERMINATION / REWARD
- None. There is no goal, no win/lose condition, and no reward rule defined.
  The game is an open-ended sandbox: click to spawn blue particles, which
  then random-walk indefinitely.
