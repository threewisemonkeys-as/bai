ADA85 — game mechanics

Grid: 11 x 11. All coordinates below are (row, col), row-first, matching how the
perception reports cells and how `click ROW COL` is addressed. (Internally the
program uses column-first Position(col, row); the facts below are stated in the
observed (row, col) frame.)

Objects (initial state):
- Suzie: single blue cell at the top-left corner, position (row 0, col 0).
    Stationary; acts as a clickable "button" (spawner).
- Billy: single red cell at the BOTTOM-left corner, position (row 10, col 0).
    Stationary; acts as a clickable "button" (spawner).
- Bottle: a 5-cell VERTICAL object in the LAST column (col 10), occupying rows
    3, 4, 5, 6, 7 of column 10. Has a boolean "broken" flag, starts false (intact).
    - Intact (broken=false), reading rows 3..7 of col 10: [white, yellow, yellow, yellow, white].
    - Broken (broken=true), reading rows 3..7 of col 10: [yellow, white, gold, white, yellow]
      — the palette flips and a gold cell appears in the MIDDLE, at (row 5, col 10).
- BottleSpot: a single white cell at (row 5, col 10). This is the rocks'
    target/destination. It sits inside the bottle (the bottle's middle cell) and is
    rendered behind the bottle, so it is never directly visible as a white cell.
- Rocks: a dynamic list, initially empty. Each Rock is a single gray cell and
    carries a boolean "breaksBottle" flag (chosen randomly true/false at spawn).
    Under seed 0 the FIRST rock spawned in an episode has breaksBottle=true.

Player actions:
- click: the ONLY meaningful action; the click LOCATION matters (the handler keys
    off which object occupies the clicked cell).
    - Click on Suzie (row 0, col 0): spawns a new Rock at (row 0, col 0) with
      breaksBottle = random true/false.
    - Click on Billy (row 10, col 0): spawns a new Rock at (row 10, col 0) with
      breaksBottle = random true/false.
    - Click on any Bottle cell (col 10, rows 3..7): if the bottle is currently
      broken, it is shown intact for THAT ONE FRAME only (broken set false); on the
      very next step it reverts to broken (see "Repair is only momentary" below).
      Clicking the bottle while it is intact does nothing.
    - Click on empty cells / anything else: no handler, no effect.
- left, right, up, down: NO handlers defined — all four are no-ops (nothing moves).
- noop: no effect (except the per-step rock movement below still runs).

Passive / per-step (clock) dynamics:
- Every step, each existing rock moves one cell toward the BottleSpot (row 5,
    col 10). Because both spawners are in column 0, the observed path is:
      1) the rock first travels horizontally along its spawn row, col 0 -> 10
         (one column per step), then
      2) it travels vertically along column 10 toward row 5 (one row per step).
    (The interpreter's unitVector steps along the larger remaining axis first;
    here the horizontal distance 10 exceeds the vertical distance 5, so the rock
    completes the horizontal leg before the vertical leg.)
- The rock is HIDDEN while it occupies the bottle's cells: as it passes col 10
    rows 3..7 it is drawn behind the bottle and is not visible (last visible just
    above/below the bottle, e.g. at (row 2, col 10) for a Suzie rock).
- A rock is also hidden on its spawn frame (it sits under Suzie/Billy at the
    corner) and first becomes visible one step later at col 1.

Bottle breaking and repair:
- When a rock with breaksBottle=TRUE reaches the BottleSpot (row 5, col 10), the
    bottle becomes broken starting the next step (gold appears at (row 5, col 10),
    palette flips as above).
- Once broken, the bottle STAYS broken indefinitely on every subsequent step — it
    never auto-repairs. (The breaking rock effectively remains at / keeps
    re-asserting the BottleSpot, so the break condition holds every step.)
- Repair is only momentary: clicking any bottle cell while broken makes it appear
    intact for exactly that single frame; on the next step the bottle reverts to
    broken. Repeated clicking only ever yields one-frame flashes of the intact
    palette.
- A rock with breaksBottle=FALSE travels to the BottleSpot the same way but does
    NOT break the bottle.

Spawn / despawn summary:
- Rocks are spawned only by clicking Suzie (from (0,0)) or Billy (from (10,0)),
    one rock per click.
- A rock disappears from view upon reaching the BottleSpot region (hidden behind
    the bottle); a breaking rock continues to hold the bottle broken thereafter.

Color changes:
- The bottle's 5 cell colors switch between the intact palette
    [white, yellow, yellow, yellow, white] and the broken palette
    [yellow, white, gold, white, yellow] (rows 3..7 of col 10) based solely on its
    broken flag. A gold cell at (row 5, col 10) is the unambiguous "broken" signal.

Win / termination / reward:
- None. There is no win condition, no score, and no termination rule defined in
    the program. It is an open-ended sandbox; all observed rewards are 0.0.
