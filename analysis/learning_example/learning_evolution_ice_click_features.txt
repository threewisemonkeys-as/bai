- On right: the movable block shifts one column right (increments column) if possible.
- On down: if the cell (1, c) is empty (no cell of any type), where c is the middle column of the movable block (the second of its three cells), a new blue/lightblue cell appears at (1, c) with color equal to the current color of the bottom block. If (1, c) is occupied, nothing happens.
