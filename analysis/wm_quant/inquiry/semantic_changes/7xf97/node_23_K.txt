The environment is a 16×16 grid (rows 0–15, columns 0–15). Static green cells occupy row 15 at odd‑numbered columns (1,3,5,…). A static mediumpurple cell is at (12,1).  
Gold and gray cells appear only in rows 0–2. They form two contiguous blocks (each block occupies a set of consecutive columns in all three rows). The two blocks are always separated by at least one empty column at the start of an episode, but actions can bring them together or change their widths and positions.  
Blue cells can appear in multiple columns and rows; they drift downward by exactly one row at every timestep regardless of the action, EXCEPT that the action `down` cancels this drift for that step (blue cells stay where they are). A blue cell that would drift below row 15 disappears.

Actions and their effects on gold and gray:

- **left**:  
  If a gray block exists, it shifts left by one column (each gray cell moves to `col‑1`). The column vacated on the right becomes empty unless it is immediately left of a gold block, in which case that column becomes gold (the gold block expands leftwards into it).  
  If no gray block exists, do nothing.

- **right**:  
  If a gray block exists, it shifts right by one column (each gray cell moves to `col+1`). The column vacated on the left becomes empty unless it is immediately right of a gold block, in which case that column becomes gold (the gold block expands rightwards into it).  
  If no gray block exists, do nothing.

- **up**: No effect on gold or gray. (Blue drift occurs as normal.)

- **down**: No effect on gold or gray. (Blue drift is cancelled.)

- **click ROW COL**:  
  * If the clicked cell is gold:  
    – If the clicked column is the rightmost column of the gold block, the entire gold block shifts left by one column. If that shift would move any gold cell to column‑‑1 (off the grid), the gold block disappears entirely.  
    – Otherwise (clicked column is not the rightmost), the entire gold block shifts right by one column. If that shift would move any gold cell past column 15, the gold block is truncated (the rightmost column of the original block is lost).  
  * If the clicked cell is gray:  
    – If a gold block currently exists, it disappears.  
    – If no gold block exists, a single‑column gold block appears at the column immediately left of the leftmost column of the gray block (i.e., at `leftmostGrayCol‑1`). If that column is less than 0, the new gold block does not appear (nothing visible changes).  
  * Clicks on empty, green, or mediumpurple cells cause no visible change to any cells.

- **noop**: No effect on gold or gray. (Blue drift occurs as normal.)

All actions listed above also cause the normal downward drift of blue cells (except `down` which cancels it). The drift always happens after the action’s own effect is applied.
