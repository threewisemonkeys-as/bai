- The red cell moves downward by 1 cell on each noop step, unless it is at row 11 or the cell directly below is occupied by any other object (including gold, darkorange, blue, or mediumpurple). Directional actions ('up', 'left', 'right') override this automatic movement on their step and produce their own displacement.
- 'up' moves the red cell upward by 4 cells; 'left' moves it left by 1 cell; 'right' moves it right by 1 cell. 'down' is assumed to move it downward by 4 cells but is not observed.
- Clicking any cell (coordinates irrelevant) instantaneously changes the current red cell to mediumpurple. That mediumpurple cell then moves upward by 1 cell each subsequent time step (on noop or any action). On the step immediately after the click, a new red cell appears at the exact location where the original red cell was before the click.
- When a red cell moves (by any means, including automatic downward or directional action) onto a cell containing a gold cell, the gold cell disappears immediately. The red cell does not appear in that cell until the next time step; on that next step, a red cell appears at that location (and the gold is permanently gone).
- The blue 2×3 block moves autonomously: each time step it shifts either left or right by 1 column. It moves right until its rightmost column reaches 11, then moves left until its leftmost column reaches 0, then moves right again, oscillating continuously. This movement is independent of the action taken.
- All other objects (darkorange L-shaped groups) are stationary and never change, except that they can block red movement if they occupy the cell immediately below the red cell (preventing automatic downward movement).
